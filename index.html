Privacy Policy

This policy applies to the Android game Hole Mart: Idle Space Tycoon (package name com.hamid.holemart), published on Google Play by the developer Hamid_hidden.

Summary

The game does not collect your personal data and does not send it to us or to anyone else. Your progress stays on your device.

Information stored on your device

The game saves the following only on your device, so that it can keep your progress:

  • Game progress, in-game currencies and upgrades.
  • Settings such as music, sound effects, vibration and language.
  • The player name and country flag you choose on the leaderboard screen, if you change them.

The game reads your device language to show the right translation. This is not sent anywhere.

Information we do not collect

The game does not ask for an account and does not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, location, advertising ID or payment details. It contains no analytics or crash-reporting service. The only Android permission it declares is internet access, and this version of the game does not use it to send data.

Leaderboard

The in-game leaderboard runs entirely on your device. Your name and score are not uploaded. The other names and scores on it are generated by the game and are not real players.

Ads and purchases

This version contains no third-party advertising and processes no real payments. Boosts in this version are free, and no money is charged.

Google Play and Android backup

Google Play handles the download and installation of the game, and Google may process data for that under the Google Privacy Policy. If Android backup is turned on for your device, Android may include the game's saved data in your personal device backup. That backup is managed by your device and Google account, and we cannot access it.

Children

We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children, because the game does not collect personal data at all.

Deleting your data

To delete your game data, uninstall the game or clear its storage in your Android settings. We hold no copy of it on any server. Backups can be managed in your Android or Google account settings.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including in the European Union, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data and to complain to your data protection authority. Because we do not receive your personal data, we normally have nothing to access or delete, but you are welcome to contact us with any request.

Changes to this policy

If a future version adds features that handle data differently, such as real ads, purchases, online leaderboards or analytics, we will update this policy before releasing that version and change the date above.
